Priority 1 of 5

Payment plan without a down payment

Today, assigning a payment plan requires the student to already be "enrolled" — but the only way to enroll is paying the down payment. That's a dead end. The fix removes the block and gives enrollment two independent doors instead of one.

Priority 2 of 5

Item store: modal, steppers, one order = one card

The buying part reuses the existing array-based submit as-is. What's new is the shopping experience — a modal with a running cart instead of a bare table — and a shared batch id that makes a multi-item checkout show up as one grouped order instead of loose, unrelated rows.

Priority 3 of 5

One cart, three settlement paths

The same cart UI from Priority 2 is reused for Parents (buying for their child) and Employees (buying for themselves) — but what happens at checkout genuinely differs, because only two of the three paths touch a student's invoice at all.

Priority 4 of 5

Item photos

No flow changes here — items get an optional image field, shown on every card built for Priorities 2 and 3. An item with no photo still renders cleanly with a placeholder, since older stock won't have one.

Priority 5 of 5

Search-first invoice lookup — extending, not replacing

The existing Collect Fees search already does "search a student, then see their fees" — it just shows the legacy fee model. A new branch detects students on the newer enrollment pipeline and renders the same balance summary used everywhere else in this plan.
